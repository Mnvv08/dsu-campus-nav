## Where to get the names

Satellite gives geometry. Names have to come from elsewhere:

- **YouTube** — search "DSU Harohalli campus tour". Walkthrough and drone videos let you match building shapes to signboards.
- **Google Maps photos** — open the single DSU Main Campus pin and browse user-uploaded images. Many show building exteriors with names on them.
- **Instagram** — the university and student club accounts post from identifiable locations.
- **dsu.edu.in** — school pages and the Harohalli hostel page carry photographs.
- **People** — seniors and classmates. Send them a screenshot of the satellite view with a numbered building and ask what it is. This is by far the fastest method and the one that produces `confirmed` entries.

## Landmarks first

Before mapping buildings, place the features that are unmistakable from above: the lake, the running track, the cricket ground, the main approach road. They anchor everything else and make it much easier to reason about which rooftop is which.

## Aliases

Record what students actually say, not what the signboard reads. Nobody types "Dr. Chandramma Dayananda Sagar Institute of Medical Education & Research Centre" — they type "hospital" or "medical college". Every alias recorded is a search that will succeed later.
